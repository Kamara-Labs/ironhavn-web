Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026  ·  Kamara Labs LLC

The short version Ironhavn is a non-custodial wallet. Your recovery phrase and private keys are generated and stored on your device only — Kamara Labs never sees them. The app has no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no cloud sync. Some network requests are made to third-party blockchain infrastructure (described below), but Kamara Labs does not collect or store your data on its own servers.

Information you provide directly

  • Wallet and account labels you choose
  • Watch-only addresses you add to track
  • Contact labels and recipient addresses you save
  • Recovery phrase material, only at the moment you create or import a wallet on-device — written to your device's Keychain and never transmitted to Kamara Labs

Information stored locally on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to Kamara Labs (Ironhavn has no backend server):

  • Wallet and account state
  • Transaction activity history
  • Browser favorites
  • Connected dApp session records and permissions
  • Security preferences (auto-lock timing, private browsing, notification settings)

Information sent to third parties

Using Ironhavn requires network requests to third-party infrastructure that Kamara Labs does not control:

Blockchain RPC providers — to read balances, estimate fees, and broadcast transactions on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, and Polygon. These requests can expose your wallet address and IP address to the RPC provider.

LI.FI — to fetch cross-chain swap quotes and route swap transactions, when you use the Swap tab.

CoinGecko — to display live token prices.

In-app browser — search queries and any site you visit are subject to those sites' own privacy practices. Ironhavn does not control what third-party sites do with your traffic.

We do not sell your data. As of this release, Ironhavn does not include any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Your controls

  • Add, rename, or remove watch-only addresses at any time
  • Review and revoke connected dApp permissions from the Security tab, per site and per permission
  • Enable private browsing mode in the browser
  • Erase the local wallet vault from your device (Security tab) — irreversible unless you have separately backed up your recovery phrase

Security

Recovery phrases and signing keys are generated and stored on-device via Apple's Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. You are solely responsible for backing up your recovery phrase and keeping it offline and private. Anyone with your recovery phrase can access your funds. Kamara Labs staff will never ask you for it.

Children's privacy

Ironhavn is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
