What Ironhavn does

Your wallets, in view. Your permissions, in your hands.

From checking a balance to reviewing an approval, keep the details that matter close at hand.

🗝️ New

Balances across wallets

See an asset’s combined balance, then choose a wallet to view its address and actions. Network labels help you keep each holding in context.

📋 New

Activity at a glance

Follow recent on-chain transfers and activity recorded in Ironhavn. See pending and failed transactions, with wallet labels and filters to find what you need.

🛡️ New

Token approval controls

Review spending allowances for tracked tokens across supported routers. Revoke an approval with device authentication and an on-chain transaction; network fees apply.

🔑

Keys that stay with you

Create or import independent wallets. Each recovery phrase is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Kamara Labs never has access to your keys.

✅ New

Verify your offline backup

Confirm selected recovery words against your offline copy before marking your backup complete. Phrase access requires authentication and automatically hides after 60 seconds.

🔒

Approve with your device

Sends and dApp signatures require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Choose an auto-lock timeout and lock your wallet whenever you need to.

🔄

Receive native Bitcoin

Share your dedicated Bitcoin address to receive BTC on the Bitcoin network. Keep Bitcoin separate from Ethereum and wrapped Bitcoin tokens.

🪙 New

Track custom tokens

Add an ERC-20 contract on a supported network. Ironhavn reads its token details on-chain and tracks its balance alongside your built-in assets.

🔤

Send using an ENS name

Enter a name.eth recipient and review the resolved wallet address before sending. Save familiar recipients in your contacts for next time.

🌐

Private dApp browsing

Explore dApps in Discover with private browsing enabled by default. Reset a session or clear stored site data, with HTTPS-only navigation and destination risk labels.

🔗

Connect by QR code

Use WalletConnect to pair with supported dApps on another screen. Saved sessions can reconnect when you reopen Ironhavn, and requests stay yours to approve or reject.

📚

Manage dApp access

Review the wallet permissions granted to connected sites and revoke browser sessions from Settings. Keep favorite dApps close with saved bookmarks.

👁️

Watch without importing keys

Add an EVM address or ENS name to track a wallet in read-only mode. Watch-only accounts let you inspect balances without bringing signing keys onto your phone.

📒

Recognize your recipients

Save verified addresses under familiar names. Optionally restrict direct sends to your own wallet and saved contacts, while reviewing each transfer before signing.

🔔

Transaction & security alerts

Enable on-device notifications for transaction outcomes and security events. Manage your alert preferences in Settings.

Protection engine

More context before you approve.

Review what a dApp is asking for, see warnings about risky permissions, and check destination details before you sign.

Readable approval warnings

Requests for unlimited token spending or access to all NFTs in a collection are flagged for review. Unrecognized contract calls show a caution message.

Transaction checks

Gas estimation checks dApp transactions before signing and surfaces detected execution failures, so you can review a problem before submitting.

Sign-in domain checks

If a Sign-In-With-Ethereum message names a different domain from the requesting site, Ironhavn warns you to verify the origin before approving.

Lookalike-site checks

Checks for lookalikes of known dApp domains help flag or block suspicious destinations. Review the site address and risk label before connecting.

Address-poisoning defense

Send recipients that mimic a recent contact's first and last characters trigger a confirmation before the transfer proceeds.

₿ Bitcoin supported

Bitcoin, natively.

Receive and send native Bitcoin (BTC) using dedicated Bitcoin addresses, with on-device signing and support for SegWit and Taproot. Your Bitcoin and Ethereum addresses are separate: always choose Bitcoin when receiving BTC.
