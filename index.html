What Ironhavn does

🔑

Non-custodial

Your recovery phrase is generated on your device and stored in the iOS Keychain. Kamara Labs never has access to it.

🔒

Auth on every action

Every send, swap, and dApp approval requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode — even during an active session.

🔄

Cross-chain swaps

Swap tokens across networks with routes aggregated by LI.FI. See fees and estimated output before you sign.

🌐

Built-in dApp browser

Browse HTTPS dApps, connect your wallet, and revoke site permissions — all from inside the app.

👁

Watch-only accounts

Track any EVM address or ENS name without importing a key. Monitor balances and activity read-only.

🛡

Phishing protection

Typosquat and lookalike domain detection, address poisoning guards, and calldata decoding on every transaction review.

No analytics. No tracking. No account required.

Ironhavn has no backend server. Your wallet state lives on your device. We don't sell your data because we don't have it.
