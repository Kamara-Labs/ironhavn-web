What Ironhavn does

Your complete self-custody toolkit.

Every feature you need to manage, protect, and grow your crypto — without giving up your keys.

🔑

Non-custodial

Your recovery phrase is generated on-device and stored in the iOS Keychain with device-presence protection. Kamara Labs never has access to it.

🗝️ New

Multiple signing wallets

Create or import any number of independent recovery phrases. Each wallet has its own device-authenticated Keychain record — fully isolated signing paths.

🔒

Auth on every action

Every send, swap, and dApp approval requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Auto-lock enforced in the background — even through the app switcher.

🔄

Cross-chain swaps

Live LI.FI quotes with real signing and broadcast. Minimal ERC-20 approvals only — the app's own risk engine flags unlimited approvals as dangerous, so it would never request one.

💸 New

Transparent swap fees

The confirmation screen breaks out every cost: Ironhavn integrator fee, LI.FI service fee, route-provider fees, and estimated network gas. No surprise line items.

🔤 New

ENS name resolution

Type a name.eth address in the Send field and Ironhavn resolves it via pure on-chain reads — no third-party API, no account needed.

🌐

Built-in dApp browser

HTTPS-only shield, EIP-1193/EIP-6963 injection, reviewed-destination risk badges, and WebKit subframe isolation so dApps can't overwrite your connection state.

🔗 New

WalletConnect v2

Connect to desktop dApps by QR code. Sessions persist across launches — the relay reopens and re-subscribes automatically on startup.

📚 New

Browser session management

Per-site connected session tracking with one-tap revoke controls. Favorites and bookmarks for the dApps you use most.

👁

Watch-only accounts

Track any EVM address or ENS name without importing a key. Signing is blocked by construction across Send, Swap, and the dApp bridge — not just hidden behind a warning.

📒 New

Address book & trusted contacts

Save recipients by name. ENS destinations resolve to their on-chain address before being checked. Optional: restrict direct sends to saved contacts only.

🔔 New

Push notifications

Local banners for transaction completion, risky dApp navigation, dangerous approvals, and blocked destinations — opt-in from the Security tab.

Protection engine

Five layers of protection on every transaction.

Most wallets show you a hex blob and ask you to trust it. Ironhavn decodes it, simulates it, and checks it for known attacks — before you ever sign.

Calldata decoding

Unlimited ERC-20 approvals and NFT setApprovalForAll grants get explicit danger banners. Unknown function selectors show a caution flag instead of raw bytes.

Pre-flight simulation

Every dApp transaction is dry-run via eth_estimateGas before you sign. "This transaction will revert" is surfaced before you pay for a doomed call.

SIWE phishing check

Sign-In-With-Ethereum messages are parsed. If the stated domain doesn't match the requesting site, the sheet is flagged as a session-hijacking attempt.

Typosquat shield

Navigation to lookalike dApp domains — uniswap.org.evil.com, app.uniswqp.org — is blocked outright, not just warned about.

Address-poisoning defense

Send recipients that mimic a recent contact's first and last characters trigger a confirmation before the transfer proceeds.

DeFi research

A DeFi tab. Built right in.

Curated protocol shortcuts, your local wallet posture, and risk labels — all from the dedicated DeFi tab. No browser extension required.
